### Thistles
 
**Artist**<br>

**Date**<br>
1883–89
 
**Medium**<br>
Oil on canvas
 
**Dimensions**<br>
55.9 × 71.8 cm (22 × 28 1/4 in.)
 
**Category**<br>
Painting
 
**Style**<br>
Impressionism
 
**Description**<br>
Beginning in 1885 and inspired by his friendship with Monet, John Singer Sargent increasingly spent time painting outside, exploring the techniques and subjects of Impressionism. Executing this work in rural England (or possibly southern France), he experimented with broken brushwork and a high-keyed palette. He continued painting outside throughout his life, a practice that provided respite from his busy schedule as an acclaimed portraitist.
